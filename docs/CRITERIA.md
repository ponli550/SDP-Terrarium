# SDP Transit: six criteria map

Rule: every figure is tagged REAL (public data, cited), SOURCED (assumption with a source) or ILLUSTRATIVE (mock). Only REAL and SOURCED go in the pitch headline. ILLUSTRATIVE is labelled on screen.

| Criterion | Weight | What the judge must see | Artifact | Status of its data |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Impact | 25% | Homes gaining a 10-minute walk to a stop, trips, time saved, as a graded range | What-if result card with three grades | stop counts REAL; trips ILLUSTRATIVE |
| Innovation | 20% | A manager runs a shuttle what-if with cost, not a static gap map | Loop editor: drag a stop, see homes covered and RM per month | cost per km SOURCED later; mock now |
| Feasibility | 20% | An 8-week one-township pilot a manager could approve | One-page pilot spec: route, vehicle, cost, who runs it | ILLUSTRATIVE until quotes |
| Strategic Fit | 15% | SDP's own words on post-handover living; replicates per township | Fit slide quoting the briefing; township switcher | quote REAL (briefing deck) |
| Commercial and Value Multiplication | 10% | Cost per rider for the manager; footfall stated as hypothesis | Cost-per-rider line; hypothesis box | ILLUSTRATIVE |
| Team, Evidence and Pitch | 10% | A validated problem and a named conversation | Evidence slide: observation notes, one conversation | EMPTY until a human does it |

Open gates: team of 3-5 with one coder, eligibility, registration by Wed 21 Oct, shuttle already existing (Elmina: none found; Bandar Bukit Raja: T728/T729 feeder exist).
