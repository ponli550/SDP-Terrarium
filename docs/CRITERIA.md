# Terrarium: the six criteria

Rule: every figure is tagged REAL (public data, cited), SOURCED (an assumption with a published source) or ILLUSTRATIVE (a placeholder to replace). Only REAL and SOURCED may be headlined in the pitch. ILLUSTRATIVE is labelled on screen.

| Criterion | Weight | What the judge must see | Built | Data status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Impact | 25% | A change in what a buyer does: less electricity, more walking | Weekly game, neighbour comparison, "what to change" suggestions | Effect size capped at about 2% (Allcott 2011, SOURCED); every other figure ILLUSTRATIVE; no measured effect |
| Innovation | 20% | Not a showroom or a gap map: a month lived before you sign, with a checked assistant | 3D township, four-week game, assistant whose figures are recomputed before display | SDP's own app has owner billing and a virtual tour, nothing on running cost (undated source) |
| Feasibility | 20% | A prototype that runs, and a pilot a manager could approve | Tested model, scene and game in the browser; real-bill matching; pilot written up in docs/PILOT.md | Pilot is a proposal; costs, partners, consent wording unconfirmed |
| Strategic Fit | 15% | SDP's own words, and its four pillars | Four pillar scores; deck quotes SDP's purpose | Slide quotes REAL; the Scope 3 mapping is unconfirmed |
| Commercial and Value Multiplication | 10% | A reason SDP gains | Hypotheses only: better buying decisions, a post-handover dashboard, PRIME link | Untested |
| Team, Evidence and Pitch | 10% | A validated problem and a named conversation | Problem-first deck with plain "not checked" statements | **EMPTY** until a recent buyer is interviewed |

Only REAL figure so far: 0.93 km from Elmina's centre to the nearest public transit stop (data.gov.my GTFS feeds; the centre is approximate and the distance is straight-line). The tariff, carbon factor, air-con and shade maths and scoring formulas are placeholders.

Built since: a first look (the moment set-up ends, a checked summary of what a month in this home costs, with one clear next action, reopenable from My month); condo levels (a condo is a unit on one of eight floors; the floor changes the cooling bill for a top-floor unit, whether plot trees help at all, and the lift time on every walk, all as ILLUSTRATIVE placeholders, and your window is marked on the tower); placing your lot on the township map (a lot sets the distance to the bus stop, scaled from the one real figure and graded ILLUSTRATIVE, and Community now scores trips on foot so living far away is never rewarded); a demo sign-in (email only, no password, nothing leaves the browser, with a guest option) that keeps the home you set up and lets you edit it; an after-handover card where an owner types the kWh on their bill and the electricity figure becomes REAL (and stops being REAL if the home changes), plus a one-page pilot proposal in docs/PILOT.md; click-to-inspect (home, neighbour, stop, bus, corridor, solar, a walker, a car) with graded rows, and a follow-a-trip camera. The camera is lazy: zoom and click-to-focus ease in, drags glide, and the browser's reduced-motion setting turns all of it off. Following covers one trip, not a resident's whole day.

Not built: the carbon analysis layer (a sourced grid factor, embodied carbon by home type), an LLM planner for the assistant, a scheduled-departures panel. The live bus feed returned nothing when tested, so any bus shown is illustrative.

Open gates: team of 3-5 with one coder, eligibility, registration by Wed 21 Oct, one recent-buyer conversation.
